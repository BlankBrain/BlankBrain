### Hi, I'm Mehedi.

I'm an agentic engineer in Dhaka. In June 2025 I started [Karigor AI Labs](https://github.com/Karigor-AI-Labs), an enterprise AI lab and platform. We build agents for organizations whose people work in Bangla, run them for those organizations, and build them to get better at their job over time.

Karigor means craftsman. Each agent we build is a karigor: it takes one job inside a business, works under written rules, and hands the work to a person where those rules say so.

**How a karigor gets better**

A karigor is not finished on the day it ships. We built a loop to keep improving it, and today that loop runs with a person in it. A karigor does real work, and we keep a record of what it did. A failure the bench finds can become a new test case for the next run. Each change to how a karigor behaves is measured before and after on KarigorBench, our own test bench, with its safety rules checked in code, and a change that does worse on them than the version it replaces does not ship. A person approves every change to how a karigor behaves before it goes live.

That loop is our main IP. Keeping it safe and visible is the research we publish.

**What we have built**

We have built and run 14 karigors on our own platform, as of 10 November 2026.

**The research record**

We publish what we find, numbered and dated: 3 findings so far and 6 studies in progress, as of 10 November 2026. Each finding says what it rests on, and is marked Amended or Corrected when a later measurement changes it. Read them at [karigor.ai/research](https://karigor.ai/research).

**Free for anyone: Karigor Skills**

We build every karigor from skills: a written procedure, rules, and test cases it has to pass. We give the skills away, packaged for ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Free under CC BY 4.0.
